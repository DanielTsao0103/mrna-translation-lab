Translation, in motion.

Double-click index.html to open the website. All visuals and data are bundled locally; no internet connection or server is needed.

Watch the mRNA thread through the ribosome. Initiation and the first two A-to-P-to-E cycles play slowly, then the cycle repeats quickly. Pause to inspect the current codon, anticodon, amino acid, and action.

The example RNA encodes the 76-residue ubiquitin monomer. The final ribbon follows the 1UBQ backbone coordinates. The folding motion is illustrative.

Translation, in motion.

Double-click index.html to open the website. All visuals and data are bundled locally; no internet connection or server is needed.

Watch the mRNA thread through the ribosome. Initiation and the first two A-to-P-to-E cycles play slowly, then the cycle repeats quickly. Choose Cell pace for 9 amino acids per second during elongation; its initiation and termination timing are illustrative. Pause to inspect the current codon, anticodon, amino acid, and action. At UAA, a release factor enters the A site and frees the chain.

The example RNA encodes the 76-residue ubiquitin monomer. The final C-alpha backbone follows 1UBQ coordinates; switch it to ribbon, heavy-atom ball-and-stick, or space-filling view. The folding motion is illustrative. The amino-acid reference includes a DNA coding triplet and free-amino-acid pKa/pI values for each representative pairing.
